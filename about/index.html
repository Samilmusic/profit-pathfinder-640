Who We Are

AL NOKHBA AL MASIYA GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C

AL NOKHBA AL MASIYA GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C is a Dubai-based company licensed for Goods Wholesalers activities in the United Arab Emirates.

Operating under the AL NOKHBA TRADING brand, the company focuses on international sourcing, wholesale procurement and the development of reliable supplier relationships.

Our approach is straightforward: identify commercially suitable products, engage directly with suppliers, evaluate the opportunity and establish a reliable purchasing channel for the UAE market.

Our Approach

How We Work with Suppliers

  1. 01

    Commercially Focused

    Every sourcing decision starts with commercial suitability: products, pricing and quantities that work for wholesale trade in the UAE.

  2. 02

    Internationally Connected

    We engage directly with manufacturers, exporters, wholesalers and stockists in established international markets, including Europe.

  3. 03

    Supplier Oriented

    Clear communication, structured evaluation and direct dialogue with suppliers — including meetings and facility visits where appropriate.

  4. 04

    Long-Term Thinking

    Our objective is not a single transaction but a reliable, continuing purchasing channel with each supplier we select.

Physical Goods

Wholesale trade in real products

Our activity is the purchase and wholesale of physical goods: professional cleaning and hygiene, hardware and technical supplies, industrial and maintenance products, building and renovation supplies, home and interior products, hospitality and catering supplies and other non-food consumer goods.
