Trading Sectors

Broad Sectors. Commercial Focus.

As a licensed goods wholesaler, AL NOKHBA sources across a broad range of non-food product categories. Our focus is on commercially viable products that can be purchased in wholesale quantities and supplied efficiently to the UAE and selected GCC markets.
