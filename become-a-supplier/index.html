Market Access

Supplier Criteria

What We Look For

  • Product Quality

    Products that meet the expectations of professional and commercial buyers.

  • Reliable Stock Availability

    Established ranges that can be supplied consistently.

  • Competitive Wholesale Pricing

    Pricing structures that support wholesale distribution.

  • Export Capability

    Experience preparing goods and documents for export.

  • Commercial Quantities

    Ability to supply pallet and container-scale volumes.

  • Reasonable Lead Times

    Practical timelines from order confirmation to dispatch.

  • Product Documentation

    Specifications, labelling and supporting product documents.

  • Long-Term Supply Potential

    Interest in a continuing commercial relationship.
