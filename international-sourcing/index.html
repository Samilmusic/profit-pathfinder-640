Sourcing Process

From first contact to UAE wholesale

Each supply opportunity follows a clear sequence. Suppliers know what to expect, what information we need and how a commercial decision is reached.

  1. 01

    Supplier Identification

    Identification of manufacturers, wholesalers, exporters and stockists offering commercially suitable products.

  2. 02

    Initial Discussion

    Introduction, product availability, export capability and commercial requirements.

  3. 03

    Catalogue & Commercial Review

    Review of product range, wholesale pricing, MOQ, stock availability and commercial terms.

  4. 04

    Product & Sample Evaluation

    Evaluation of selected products and samples where required.

  5. 05

    Direct evaluation

    Supplier Visit

    Where commercially appropriate, our management may visit the supplier's headquarters, showroom, factory or warehouse to evaluate products and discuss the potential cooperation directly.

    Seeing products, stock and operations first-hand allows both parties to confirm quality, packaging, availability and logistics before significant purchasing commitments are made.

  6. 06

    Commercial Negotiation

    Final discussion regarding quantities, pricing, Incoterms, availability and payment conditions.

  7. 07

    Purchase Order

    Formal wholesale purchase following commercial approval.

  8. 08

    Export & UAE Import

    Coordination of export documentation, logistics and import into the United Arab Emirates.

  9. 09

    Wholesale, B2B & Marketplace Distribution

    Following product entry and commercial evaluation, products are distributed through AL NOKHBA's wholesale, B2B and selected online marketplace channels — primarily within the UAE, with selected opportunities in GCC markets.

Information Requested

What we typically request from suppliers

To evaluate a new opportunity efficiently, our procurement team usually asks for the following during the initial discussion and commercial review.

  • Product catalogue
  • Wholesale price list
  • Current stock availability
  • Minimum order quantities (MOQ)
  • Export terms & Incoterms
  • Sample availability
  • Product documentation & specifications
  • Indicative lead times

Established Markets

European and international suppliers

We focus on suppliers in established international markets — with particular attention to European manufacturers, wholesalers and stockists offering consistent quality, documentation and export experience.
