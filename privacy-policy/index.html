This Privacy Policy explains how AL NOKHBA AL MASIYA GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C (“AL NOKHBA”, “we”, “us”), operating under the AL NOKHBA TRADING brand, handles information received through alnokhbatrading.com and related business correspondence. This website is intended for business-to-business use.

01Who we are

AL NOKHBA AL MASIYA GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C is a company registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates (Trade Licence No. 1408620), with its registered address at SaiH Shuaib 3, Plot 13, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy matter, contact us at Manager@alnokhbatrading.com.

02Information we collect

We collect information that you choose to provide to us, including:

  • Business contact details such as your name, job title, company name, country, company website, business email address and telephone / WhatsApp number;
  • Commercial information about your company and products, such as product categories, brands, export markets, MOQ, lead times, stock availability and indicative order values;
  • Documents you upload or send to us, such as catalogues, product lists and price lists;
  • The content of messages and correspondence you send to us by email, telephone or WhatsApp.

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may also process limited technical data (such as IP address, browser type and request logs) for security and reliability purposes.

03How we use information

We use the information we receive to:

  • Evaluate supplier proposals and potential sourcing and purchasing opportunities;
  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you about commercial discussions, meetings and visits;
  • Prepare, negotiate and administer purchase orders and related trade documentation;
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of this website;
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations in the United Arab Emirates.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing purposes.

04Legal basis

We process business contact information in connection with taking steps at your request prior to entering into a commercial relationship, for our legitimate business interests in sourcing and procurement, and where required to comply with the law, in line with applicable UAE data protection legislation.

05Sharing of information

Information is accessed by AL NOKHBA personnel involved in procurement and supplier relations. We may share information with service providers who support our business operations (such as website hosting, email delivery and logistics or freight partners when coordinating shipments), and with authorities where required by law. Service providers may process information outside the United Arab Emirates.

06Third-party services on this website

This website loads web fonts and photographs from third-party content delivery networks, and links to external services such as WhatsApp. When you use those services, their own privacy policies apply.

07Retention

We keep supplier and enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate and manage the business relationship, and thereafter as required for legal, accounting or record-keeping purposes.

08Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

09Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or object to its processing, by writing to Manager@alnokhbatrading.com.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the latest update shown above.
