Order Formats

Pallets, consolidated shipments and containers

Depending on the product category, procurement can include palletised goods, consolidated shipments and container-scale orders.

Commercial terms — including quantities, Incoterms, payment conditions and supply schedules — are agreed directly with each supplier before a purchase order is issued.

Our Route to Market

From International Suppliers
to the UAE & GCC Market

Products sourced by AL NOKHBA are purchased for commercial distribution primarily within the United Arab Emirates, with selected opportunities across GCC markets.

Our route-to-market strategy combines wholesale distribution, B2B sales, established commercial relationships and selected online marketplace channels.

  1. 01

    Wholesale Distribution

    Supply of products in commercial quantities to businesses, traders and professional buyers within the UAE.

  2. 02

    B2B Commercial Sales

    Direct sales to business customers through our commercial network and established market connections.

  3. 03

    Online Marketplaces

    Selected consumer-facing products may also be distributed through established online marketplaces such as Amazon UAE and other appropriate UAE/GCC e-commerce channels, complementing our core wholesale and B2B activities.

  4. 04

    GCC Opportunities

    Where commercially appropriate, selected products may be supplied to business customers and trading partners in GCC markets.

Online marketplaces are an additional channel for selected consumer-facing products; AL NOKHBA's core activity remains wholesale and B2B distribution.
